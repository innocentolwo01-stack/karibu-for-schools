# Premium QA checklist

## First launch
1. Install the preview APK.
2. Confirm the black native splash displays the Karibu mark.
3. Confirm welcome illustration, copy and buttons fit without clipping.
4. Complete all four onboarding pages.
5. Close and reopen the app; onboarding should remain completed.

## Authentication
1. Test parent, student and teacher role selection.
2. Confirm school code, ID and password validation.
3. Confirm each role opens its correct dashboard.
4. Confirm logout and returning login.

## Navigation
1. Move through every bottom tab.
2. Confirm tab state and scroll position remain stable.
3. Test Android back navigation from nested screens.
4. Confirm notifications and shortcuts deep-link correctly.

## Parent
1. Switch between both children.
2. Open payments and make successful, pending and failed sandbox payments.
3. Confirm balance/history/receipt states update.
4. Book Mathematics, English and Biology teachers.
5. Test video, phone and in-person meeting types.
6. Add a meeting to the calendar, reschedule it, cancel it and book another.
7. Complete the simulated meeting flow.

## Student
1. Open timetable, assignments, results and learning resources.
2. Change an assignment from not started to submitted.
3. Test the QR attendance scan journey.
4. Open digital ID, library and cafeteria modules.

## Teacher
1. Complete a manual register.
2. Start QR attendance and test live student scans.
3. Create and mark an assignment.
4. Enter and publish results.
5. Review parent appointments.

## Device quality
- No white flashes during navigation
- No clipped cards or tab labels
- Keyboard does not cover important form controls
- Buttons show pressed/loading/disabled feedback
- Success, pending, failure and empty states remain readable
- App state survives a restart
